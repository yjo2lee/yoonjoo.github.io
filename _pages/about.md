I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Michigan CSE](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/), working with [Q. Vera Liao](https://qveraliao.com/) on human-centered AI. I received my Ph.D. in [Computer Science](https://cs.kaist.ac.kr/) from [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/), where I worked with [Juho Kim](https://juhokim.com/) as a member of [KIXLAB](https://www.kixlab.org/). During my Ph.D., I interned at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/) with [Alexa F. Siu](https://alexasiu.com/) and [Tong Sun](https://research.adobe.com/person/tong-sun), at the [Allen Institute for AI](https://allenai.org/) with [Pao Siangliulue](https://paoponder.com/), [Joseph Chee Chang](https://josephcc.com/), [Jonathan Bragg](https://www.jonathanbragg.com/), and [Kyle Lo](https://kyleclo.com/), and at [LG AI Research](https://www.lgresearch.ai/) with [Moontae Lee](https://moontae.github.io/).

My research lies at the intersection of **human-AI interaction** and **natural language processing**, centered on **cognitive alignment**: building AI systems that understand, adapt to, and augment how people reason, learn, and work. I pursue this through three complementary directions: (1) developing **AI systems that adapt their support** to individual users' tasks, contexts, and evolving needs; (2) designing **evaluation frameworks and benchmarks** that measure AI capabilities in fine-grained, human-interpretable ways; and (3) building **user simulators** that enable scalable evaluation and training by modeling how users interact with and respond to AI.
